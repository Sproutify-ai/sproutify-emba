Expert Evaluation Instructions

Overview

You will evaluate 24 innovation submissions using the same 5 criteria that were used in the original screening process. Your task is to determine the "correct" pass/fail decision for each submission based on these specific rules.

Key Differences from the Original Screening Process

  • Original Process: Screeners evaluated criteria in order (1→5) and could stop at first failure
  • Your Task: Evaluate ALL criteria for each submission, regardless of failures on earlier criteria

Why This Difference?

  • We need to understand which specific criteria each solution fails
  • Your ratings will serve as "ground truth" for evaluating screener performance

Evaluation Process

Step 1: Read Each Submission Carefully

Step 2: Evaluate Each Criterion Independently

For each of the 5 criteria, make a binary decision:

  • ✅ PASS: Solution clearly meets this criterion
  • ❌ FAIL: Solution does not meet this criterion

Step 3: Provide an Evaluation for Each Criterion

For every criterion, provide a reasoning explaining your thought process.

  • Evaluate all criteria: Even if a solution clearly fails Criterion 1, still evaluate Criteria 2-5
  • Ensure you thoroughly assess the criterion; the goal is to screen out and flag solutions that do not meet the criterion.
